Jamie

Age: 26 Location: Ontario

University graduate living in Oshawa, ON, looking for employment. Jamie graduated with a Masters degree in AI from the University of Toronto.

She moved from downtown Toronto to Oshawa after completing her degree with her friends. She is currently managing her living expenses with a part-time job and has a student debt that will start in six months, so she is actively looking for full-time employment to cover it.

Needs: Jamie needs a decent-paying job and a student debt relief plan. Without financial freedom she feels overwhelmed and worries that AI is taking away the jobs of fresh graduates and junior positions.

Catherine

Age: 28 Location: Ontario

Catherine pursued her Masters in Business Analytics & AI in Oshawa and secured a full-time position as a Data Analyst at J.P. Morgan in Toronto.

She took advantage of AI and strengthened her profile by learning AI tools and agents, which helped her stand out.

Needs: Catherine is unable to pay off all her debt with her present package and wants to level up and earn a promotion to Senior Analyst so she can give her parents some rest. She actively uses AI tools to improve her skills and performance in her profession.
